Set up your own sheet

Your own private copy of Credit Card Expenses: your data lives in your Google Sheet, nobody else can see it. About 10 minutes on a computer, once.

1Create a blank Google Sheet

  1. Open sheets.new (signed in to your Google account). A blank sheet opens.
  2. Give it a name at the top left, e.g. Expenses.

Signed in to several Google accounts? Do all of this in a private / incognito window with only one account, or Apps Script may refuse to open.

2Paste the script

This page made you a private key: a long random password only your app and your sheet will know. It is already written into the code below, and never leaves this page.

…
  1. In the sheet: Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Click into the editor, select everything (Ctrl+A / ⌘A), and paste (Ctrl+V / ⌘V).
  3. Save (Ctrl+S / the disk icon).

3Build the sheet (one click)

  1. In the toolbar, pick setupNewSheet from the function list, then press Run.
  2. Google asks for permission: Review permissions → pick your account → Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) → Allow.
    It says “unsafe” only because the script is yours and not reviewed by Google. It can only touch this one sheet.
  3. The log says Ready. Your sheet now has Categories, Summary, Accounts and this month’s tab.

Optional: type your current balance into cell A2 of the month tab. Categories can be changed later, in the sheet or from the app.

4Publish it as a web app

  1. Deploy → New deployment. Click the gear next to “Select type” and choose Web app.
  2. Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone.
    “Anyone” only means the app can reach it; without your key every request is refused.

✓Later: updating the script

When a new feature needs it, paste the newest script again but keep your own var API_KEY = '…'; line, then Deploy → Manage deployments → ✏️ → New version → Deploy. Choose “New version”, not “New deployment”, so the address stays the same.
