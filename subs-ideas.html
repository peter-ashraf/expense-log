★ My pick Wallet + strip + rating

A strip of days on top (tap a day with a dot), a stack of cards below (tap one to bring it forward, tap again to flip it), and a quick rating on each card.

1 · Card wallet

Like Apple Wallet. Tap a card to pull it out of the stack, tap it again to flip it over, tap the background to put it back.

Every month

2 · Renewal calendar

Each renewal drops in as a coin on its day; expensive days glow. Tap a day to see what is charged.

3 · Physics bubbles

Bubble size = yearly cost. Drag and fling them; tilt your phone after tapping “Use tilt”. Drop one on the bin to skip it this month (tap “Reset” to bring them back).

Skip this month

4 · Treemap

Area = share of your fun money, grouped by kind. Tap a group to zoom in, tap “All” to zoom out.

All subscriptions

5 · Timeline ribbon

The next 30 days, scroll sideways. Logos sit on their renewal day, the line climbs with what you will have paid.

6 · Is it worth it?

Rate each one: 🔥 love it, 😐 meh, 💤 barely use it. The bar shows where your money goes, and 💤 ones become cancel candidates with the yearly saving.
